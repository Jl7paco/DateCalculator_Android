# Privacy Policy for DateCalculator (日期计算器)

**Last Updated**: September 19, 2026

**DateCalculator** ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information.

---

## 1. Information Collection & Usage

### 📍 Location Data (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` / `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`)
- **Purpose**: If you enable the optional "GPS Auto-Detect Region" setting, the app accesses your device's coarse or fine location solely to determine your country code (e.g. US, CN, JP) and automatically select the corresponding statutory holiday rules.
- **Storage & Transmission**: Your location data is processed 100% locally on your device in real-time. We **DO NOT** record, store, or transmit your location data to any external server or third party.

### 🔔 Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`)
- **Purpose**: Used solely to deliver local on-device countdown reminder notifications that you explicitly create.
- **Storage & Transmission**: Notifications are generated on-device via Android system APIs. No personal data is sent externally.

### 💾 Calculation Logs & Personal Data
- All date calculation logs, custom countdown events, and preferences are saved locally on your device. We do not collect, track, or sell any personal data.

---

## 2. Third-Party Services
Our application operates completely offline-first and does not integrate third-party tracking, advertising, or analytics SDKs.

---

## 3. Children's Privacy
DateCalculator does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
