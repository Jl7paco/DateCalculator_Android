# Privacy Policy for Date Calculator (日期计算器)

**Last Updated:** September 24, 2024  
**Effective Date:** September 24, 2024  

This Privacy Policy applies to the **Date Calculator** mobile application (hereinafter referred to as "the App"), developed by **Paco** ("we", "us", or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data of our users.

---

## 1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

### A. Location Data (GPS)
- **Usage:** The App requests Location Permissions (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` and `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`) solely to detect your current city and fetch accurate real-time weather forecasts, as well as to allow you to perform 1-click location check-ins.
- **Processing:** Location data is processed locally on your device or used briefly to query public weather APIs (such as Open-Meteo). We do not track, log, or sell your precise real-time location.

### B. Device & Storage Data
- **Usage:** All your calculated dates, anniversaries, countdowns, theme preferences, and check-in records are stored **locally on your device** using Android `SharedPreferences`.
- **Processing:** We do not upload your personal events, notes, or calculation histories to any external server.

---

## 2. Third-Party Services

The App communicates with minimal third-party network services:
- **Open-Meteo API:** Used exclusively to retrieve weather forecasts based on latitude/longitude coordinates. No Personally Identifiable Information (PII) is transmitted.

---

## 3. Data Safety & Security

- **No Ads:** The App contains zero advertising SDKs.
- **No Data Selling:** We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data with any third parties.
- **Local Control:** You can clear all app history and stored data at any time from within the App's settings or by clearing the App's storage via Android system settings.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
